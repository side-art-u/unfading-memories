Unfading Memories

Persephone, a fearless paranormal investigator, is sent to a haunted mall to get rid of a spirit. However, she also has a personal mission: to find answers for what happened to her missing brother, and a keepsake to remember him by before she leaves for college. Inside the mall, she finds Lucius, a volatile, child-like ghost who tries to keep her trapped so he can forcefully make a friend. Things quickly escalate as Lucius's anger over Persephone's attempted escape grows, forcing her to make a difficult choice between clinging to her past or letting go.

CHARACTER DOSSIER #01
PERSEPHONE
17-YEAR-OLD PARANORMAL INVESTIGATOR

A 17-year-old fearless paranormal investigator who explores an abandoned mall. She is a logical person that knows how to piece things together. However, her flaws make it so she tends to obsess over finding clean answers, convinced that if she works hard enough, she will get what she’s looking for one way or another.

In spite of all this, she has an overall lighthearted personality. Her strengths lie in her wits and quick-thinking that allow her to adapt to changes and think of solutions under pressure.

CHARACTER DOSSIER #02
LUCIUS
TRAPPED SPIRIT OF A 10-YEAR-OLD

The trapped spirit of a 10-year-old child who went missing. He remains naive and playful as he was at the time of his death, almost as if his spirit stayed frozen in time. He is currently confined to the space of an empty abandoned mall, and his goal is to trap any unsuspecting passerby inside so he can have some company.

STUDIO PROFILE

Side Art Studios is operated by three newly-emerging creators who value stories made with passion and care. With our combined skills, we are able to work with 2D art, character design, environment design, prop design, illustrations, and 3D art. We exist to work alongside independent creators so we can provide a complete concept development pipeline at a fraction of the normal cost. In short, we take raw concepts and turn them into a marketable and professional fully-realized IP’s made with passion and high quality in mind, so audiences of all ages can enjoy good stories.
